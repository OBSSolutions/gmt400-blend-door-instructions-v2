Installation Guide

Follow all four sections in order for best results

① Blend Door Seals ② Trimming ③ Gluing ④ Shaft Seals
1
Blend Door Seals

This kit comes with 2 seals for each of the 3 blend doors. Identify the correct seals for each door before proceeding.

  • 1
    Plastic Blend Door
    The largest pair
  • 2
    Large Metal Door
  • 3
    Small Metal Door
    Smallest Pair
Fig. 1 — Blend door seal identification overview
✔ Lay out all seals and match them to the correct door before beginning installation.
2
Trimming
⛔ TRIMMING IS REQUIRED — DO NOT SKIP!! Any interference will cause binding and prevent the blend door from moving freely.

Before installing the blend doors, ensure that there is sufficient clearance between the housing and the seal near the shaft.

Fig. 2 — Where interference can occur near the shaft

The photo above shows where interference can occur. The photo below shows an example of trimming that allows clearance.

Fig. 3 — Example of trimming for clearance

For accurate alignment, it's best to carry out trimming after installing the shaft seals.

⚠ ENSURE ALL DOORS MOVE WITHOUT ANY BINDING BEFORE GLUING!
3
Gluing

Once trimming is finished and you're set to complete the installation, it's strongly advised to apply glue inside the blend door seals before placing them onto the blade.

⚠ Applying glue inside is especially important for the plastic door due to the raised fins.
Fig. 4 — Seal placement and gluing reference

Please ensure all components are properly fitted prior to reinstallation. Trim the outer edges where necessary for optimal results.

✔ Confirm all doors move freely before applying adhesive. Once glued, repositioning is difficult.
4
Shaft Seals
  • 1
    Seals for Large Metal Door
  • 2
    Seals for Small Metal Door
  • 3
    Seal for Plastic Door (only one — non-removal arm)

These are necessary if you have a loose or disconnected door, or are completely removing the blend doors from the housing.

Fig. 5 — Shaft seal identification (1, 2, 3)

From the factory, the doors depend on the seal to stay aligned. The metal doors are known to fall out when this material has worn away. It is highly recommended to use the seals if the factory material has worn off.

Fig. 6 — Example of an installed seal around the shaft

The box's external linkage is press-fitted and removable. If broken, replacements are available online with replacement springs.

⚠ If replacing the linkage, ensure you replace it with the same arm design. The examples below are for visual reference only and are not included in this kit.
