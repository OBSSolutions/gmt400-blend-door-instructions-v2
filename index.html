Identify Your Seals

This kit comes with 6 seals: 2 for each of the 3 blend doors. Every seal has its part number raised on both faces. Match each seal to its door before you begin.

Part #1 — Plastic Blend Door SealQty 2 per kit (the same part is used for both). The rib cut-out goes at the shaft (hub) end.
Large Metal Door
Part #2 — Large Metal Door Seal, Narrow SideQty 1. No clip notch, so either end works.
Part #3 — Large Metal Door Seal, Wide SideDIRECTIONALQty 1. The deeper cut (the shaft clip end) goes on the housing clip side. See Orientation in section 2.
Small Metal Door
Part #4 — Small Metal Door SealQty 1. No clip notch, so either end works.
Part #5 — Small Metal Door Seal, Clip EndDIRECTIONALQty 1. The deeper cut (the shaft clip end) goes on the housing clip side. See Orientation in section 2.

✔ Lay out all seals and match them to the correct door before beginning installation.
2
Gluing

These seals are made to fit as supplied — no trimming is needed. It is strongly advised to glue the blend door seals onto the blade, applying glue inside the seal before placing it on the blade.

⚠ Applying glue inside is especially important for the plastic door due to the raised fins.
🧴 Adhesive is not included in this kit. Buy it separately before you start.

Use 3M™ Super Weatherstrip and Gasket Adhesive, part number 08008 (black). It is made to bond rubber weatherstripping and gaskets to metal.

  • 1
    Dry fit
    Slide each seal onto its blade without glue. Confirm it seats fully and the door moves freely. Check the orientation of parts #3 and #5 (see below).
  • 2
    Clean
    Wipe dust and debris off the blade and out of the seal. 3M recommends using this adhesive with its General Purpose Adhesive Cleaner (08984) or Gasket Prep and Parts Cleaner (08901).
  • 3
    Apply
    Squeeze the adhesive from the tube and brush it on each surface being bonded: the inside of the seal and the door blade.
  • 4
    Seat
    3M lists a work time of 1–5 minutes, so glue and seat one seal at a time. Press the seal fully onto the blade and wipe away any excess.
  • 5
    Cure
    Allow up to 24 hours to fully cure before moving the door or reassembling.
⛔ ORIENTATION MATTERS FOR PARTS #3 AND #5. How these two seals face depends on where the housing clip is. The side with the deeper cut (the shaft clip end) goes on the side with the housing clip.
