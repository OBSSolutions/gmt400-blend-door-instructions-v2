Glue the seals on

These seals are made to fit as supplied, so no trimming is needed. It is strongly advised to glue the blend door seals onto the blade, applying glue inside the seal before placing it on the blade.

Applying glue inside is especially important for the plastic door due to the raised fins.

Not included
Use this adhesive3M™ Super Weatherstrip and Gasket Adhesive
Part number 08008 (black)

It is made to bond rubber weatherstripping and gaskets to metal.

Adhesive is not included in this kit. Buy it separately before you start.

  1. 1

    Dry fit

    Slide each seal onto its blade without glue. Confirm it seats fully and the door moves freely. Check the orientation of parts #3 and #5 (see below).

  2. 2

    Clean

    Wipe dust and debris off the blade and out of the seal. 3M recommends using this adhesive with its General Purpose Adhesive Cleaner (08984) or Gasket Prep and Parts Cleaner (08901).

  3. 3

    Apply

    Squeeze the adhesive from the tube and brush it on each surface being bonded: the inside of the seal and the door blade.

  4. 4

    Seat

    3M lists a work time of 1–5 minutes, so glue and seat one seal at a time. Press the seal fully onto the blade and wipe away any excess.

  5. 5

    Cure

    Allow up to 24 hours to fully cure before moving the door or reassembling.

Orientation matters for parts #3 and #5

How these two seals face depends on where the housing clip is. The side with the deeper cut (the shaft clip end) goes on the side with the housing clip.

Read and follow the warnings and safety information on the adhesive tube before use.

Confirm all doors move freely before applying adhesive. Once glued, repositioning is difficult.
