Clean and glue the seals on

These seals are made to fit as supplied, so no trimming is needed. The doors can stay in the housing. It is strongly advised to glue the blend door seals onto the blade. Apply glue to the inside of the seal and to the blade before placing the seal on.

Applying glue inside is especially important for the plastic door due to the raised fins.

Not included
OBS Solutions recommended3M™ Super Weatherstrip and Gasket Adhesive
Part number 08008 (black)

It is made to bond rubber weatherstripping and gaskets to metal.

For 08008, 3M lists a work time of 1–5 minutes and a cure time of up to 24 hours, and recommends its General Purpose Adhesive Cleaner (08984) or Gasket Prep and Parts Cleaner (08901).

Adhesive is not included in this kit. Buy it separately before you start. If you use a different adhesive, follow its instructions.

Cleaning is critical for proper fit. Our seals are engineered to fit each door precisely. Any old material left on the door can get in the way and cause air leaks.

  1. 1

    Remove the old seal material

    Remove all of the old seal from the blade and from around the shaft, down to the base metal or plastic. Check every door before you continue.

How clean is clean

  1. Find the old material

    Look for dry, hardened, or crumbling seal material still stuck to the door.

  2. Scrape and pick it off

    Keep removing it until you reach the base metal or plastic.

  3. Stop at the bare door

    Check the whole blade and around the shaft. Nothing old should be left.

Orientation matters for parts #3 and #5

How these two seals face depends on where the housing clip is. The side with the deeper cut (the shaft clip end) goes on the side with the housing clip.

  1. 2

    Test fit first

    With no glue, slide each seal onto its blade and confirm it seats fully. Check the orientation of parts #3 and #5 (see above). Move the door through its full range of travel. It must move freely.

Test fit every seal and move every door before you glue. The doors move on spring tension only, so any binding can keep a door from moving. Do not apply adhesive until each door moves freely with its seals on. Once glued, repositioning is difficult.

  1. 3

    Apply the adhesive

    Follow your adhesive's instructions. Brush it on the inside of the seal and on the door blade.

Keep glue out of the shaft area. The shaft has to turn freely. Wipe away any glue that gets near it right away.

  1. 4

    Seat and clamp

    Glue and seat one seal at a time, within your adhesive's work time. Press the seal fully onto the blade, wipe away any excess, then clamp it so it stays pressed in place.

  1. 5

    Cure

    Leave the clamps on until the adhesive has cured for the time its manufacturer recommends. Then remove the clamps. Confirm the door and shaft still move freely before moving on.

Clamp it while it sets

  1. Press and clamp

    Seat the seal fully, then clamp it so it stays pressed onto the blade.

  2. Cure, then unclamp

    Leave it clamped until the adhesive has cured as its manufacturer recommends. Then take the clamps off.

Read and follow the warnings and safety information on the adhesive tube before use.
